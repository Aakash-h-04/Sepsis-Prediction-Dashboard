Central Nervous System: Sepsis-Associated Encephalopathy (SAE)

Neurological Dysfunction

Cytokines (TNF-α, IL-1β) breach the blood-brain barrier. Microglial activation and cerebral microcirculatory hypoperfusion cause neuroinflammation, delirium, acute confusion, and altered consciousness.

Clinical Laboratory Biomarkers Glasgow Coma Scale < 15, Elevated S100B Diagnostic assessment markers
Physiological Vital Signs Lethargy, Confusion, Disorientation Bedside patient presentation
