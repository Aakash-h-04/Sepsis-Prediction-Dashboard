KNOW THE WARNING SIGNS

Do not wait for every symptom to appear.

Sepsis can look different from person to person. Warning signs are educational information, not a self-diagnosis checklist.

!
Suspected sepsis or a person who is seriously unwell needs urgent medical assessment.

Contact local emergency services or seek urgent care. Do not delay to complete an online form or ask a chatbot.

Possible warning signs

  • New confusion, disorientation, or difficulty staying awake.
  • Difficulty breathing or unusually rapid breathing.
  • Severe discomfort, pain, or a feeling of being extremely unwell.
  • Clammy or sweaty skin.
  • Rapid heart rate or a weak pulse.
  • Fever, shivering, feeling very cold, or unusually low body temperature.
  • Very little urine or other signs of serious deterioration.

These signs can also occur in other illnesses. Only a qualified medical professional can assess the situation using the overall clinical context and appropriate examinations or tests.

Why vital-sign values are not enough

Heart rate, temperature, and oxygen saturation can be influenced by many conditions, devices, activity, medication, measurement error, and individual variation. A model using only these inputs cannot replace clinical assessment.

What this project can and cannot do

The current SepsisSense model is an experimental engineering demonstration with weak evaluation metrics and no clinical validation. It must not be used to screen, diagnose, treat, or rule out sepsis.
