Clinical Differential

Common Infection vs. Sepsis Emergency

Key signs that differentiate common viral illnesses from emergent systemic deterioration.

Alertness & Cognition
Common Flu: Tired, headache Sepsis: Slurred speech, acute confusion
Respiratory Pattern
Common Flu: Cough, nasal congestion Sepsis: Rapid shallow panting (>22/min)
Circulation & Skin
Common Flu: Warm, flushed skin Sepsis: Mottled, patchy, cold extremities
Urinary Output
Common Flu: Mildly reduced Sepsis: No urine for over 12 hours (AKI)
