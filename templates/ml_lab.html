ENGINEERING & INTERVIEW PREPARATION

Technical Deep Dive: Design Decisions & Trade-Offs

Q1: Why did you choose a decision threshold of 0.01 instead of 0.50?

In clinical triage, false negatives are catastrophic (missing an impending septic shock event), whereas false positives trigger clinical review. Under extreme class imbalance (~2% positive rate), standard 0.50 thresholds produce near-zero sensitivity. Threshold 0.01 was empirically selected on the validation set to achieve ~55% recall.

Q2: How do you prevent patient-level data leakage during training?

Splitting hourly ICU rows randomly causes severe data leakage because consecutive hours from the same patient share identical physiological baselines. We enforced group-based splitting on PatientID so no patient's data ever spans across train, validation, and test partitions.
