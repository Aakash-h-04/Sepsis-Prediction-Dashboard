Interpretation

The recorded test precision is low, and the ROC-AUC indicates weak discrimination. The threshold has a major impact on the number of positive flags. This evidence is not sufficient to establish clinical performance, generalization, or pre-onset warning.

Reproducibility and limits

The metadata records the experiment configuration and summary results. Reproducing the experiment would also require the exact original data pipeline, split assignments, package environment, and training code. The training dataset is not deployed with the web service.
